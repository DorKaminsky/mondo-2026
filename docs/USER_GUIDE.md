## 🎯 How predictions work

Every match has **5 separate things** to predict. Get each one right, get points. There is no "all or nothing" — you can be partially right and still score.

### Per match — 5 bets

For each match you pick:
1. **Match result** — Home win / Draw / Away win
2. **Home team goals** — exact number (e.g. 2)
3. **Away team goals** — exact number (e.g. 1)
4. **First scoring team** — Home / Away / None (for 0-0)
5. **Goal difference** — exact difference (e.g. 1)

### Points per correct bet

| Stage | Points per correct bet | Max per match |
|---|---|---|
| Group stage | 2 pts | 10 (5 of 5 = "perfect ⭐") |
| Knockout (R32 → Final) | 3 pts | 15 (perfect ⭐ in KO) |

**Perfect match** = all 5 bets correct. A ⭐ shows up next to your name on the leaderboard for each one. Worth bragging about.

### Example
You predict: **Brazil 2-1 France**, first scorer Brazil.
Actual: **Brazil 2-1 France**, first goal by Brazil.
→ All 5 bets correct → **10 pts** (group stage) and one ⭐.

You predict: **Brazil 3-2 France**, first scorer Brazil.
Actual: **Brazil 2-1 France**, first goal by Brazil.
→ Result ✓, home goals ✗, away goals ✗, first scorer ✓, goal diff ✓ → **6 pts** (3 of 5 correct × 2).

- **Each match prediction** locks **1 hour before kickoff**.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I change my prediction after I save it?**
Yes — any time before the deadline (1h before kickoff for matches, tournament start for pre-tournament). The latest save wins.

**Q: I forgot to predict a match. What happens?**
A default 0-0 draw is filled in for you, scored at half rate (1 pt per correct bet instead of 2/3). You don't lose the entire match, just the multiplier and the chance at a perfect ⭐.

**Q: Can I be in two leagues?**
One league per email. If you want to play in two pools, register again with a different email + that pool's invite code.

**Q: My invite code says "Invalid".**
Codes are 6 characters from `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789` (no zero, no O, no I, no 1). They're case-insensitive but must be exactly 6 chars. The code for our league is **`J6WEVG`** — copy-paste it to be safe.

**Q: I forgot my password.**
There's no self-service password reset (yet). Ping Dor on WhatsApp and he'll reset it manually in the database.

**Q: Why does the app sometimes look slightly outdated?**
PWAs cache the previous version aggressively. Pull-to-refresh once and you're on the latest. (Or close + reopen the app.)

**Q: Can I see what my friends predicted?**
Not until **after the deadline passes** for that match. Then a "group opinion" widget on the home page shows how the league voted (Home/Draw/Away percentages). No spoilers before kickoff.

**Q: What time zone is everything in?**
Match times are shown in **your local time** (with a `GMT±N` label so it's unambiguous). Deadlines are anchored to UTC kickoff times — `kickoff − 1 hour`, regardless of where you are.

**Q: Is there a mobile app on the App Store?**
No, and there won't be. It's a PWA — installs to your home screen and behaves identically. App Store costs $99/year and adds zero features.

**Q: What does the prize pool look like?**
That's between you and Dor. The platform doesn't handle money — settle however you usually do (Bit / PayBox / Venmo / cash / a beer). Dor decides the buy-in and tracks who paid.
